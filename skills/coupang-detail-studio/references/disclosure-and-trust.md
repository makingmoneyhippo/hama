# Product information, reviews, and the Keyfriend brand

## Category-specific disclosure

The [national-law listing for the electronic-commerce product information notice](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/admRulInfoR.do?admRulSeq=2100000213455) calls for information appropriate to the product category and transaction terms before purchase. The user's final designed page panel is a compact **five-field product/size card**, not a substitute for the applicable seller-listing disclosure. Keep any additional category-required information in the separate ledger and actual Coupang listing workflow.

For each SKU, create a disclosure ledger with `field`, `required for chosen category?`, `value`, `evidence`, `where displayed`, and `status`. Determine the category first: accessories, kitchenware, auto goods, clothing, electrical goods, children's products, food, and other goods have different field sets. Do not replace the category list with a three-item universal template.

The final designed panel contains only 브랜드, 제품명, 소재, 색상/옵션, 사이즈. Confirm these before buyer-facing page production, including what each measurement represents. Separately inspect country of manufacture, intended use, age suitability, and warnings for the actual category listing. Use `제조국: 중국` or `사용 연령: 만 14세 이상` only with SKU-level evidence and product-classification support. Dimensions, size ranges, colors, material, and contents must match the chosen option. A supplier image, packaging, invoice, product label, or measured sample can support an entry; record which. A 1688 listing assertion remains seller-stated until corroborated.

Manufacturer/importer or other category-required entries may be inconvenient to obtain. Do not fabricate or drop them from the listing workflow. Keep unresolved items in an internal missing-evidence list; do not append extra rows to the five-field designed panel by default. Do not call the page publication-ready until the required fields are resolved and the exact Coupang seller form is checked.

## Reviews and customer voice

The [current official recommendation/endorsement review guideline](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/admRulInfoR.do?admRulSeq=2100000280130) (effective June 2026) states that a consumer endorsement describing experience must come from an actual user and match the real experience. It also addresses AI-generated virtual endorsers. Use genuine reviews only for the same product or option, preserve their meaning, identify the source/date when available, and disclose incentives where applicable. Do not invent a customer name, star rating, purchase badge, usage period, or praise and present it as a review.

If authentic reviews are missing, use panel 15 for a **사용 예시**, common buyer question, or FAQ. Do not add a routine AI-generation badge to product imagery. A staged scene must remain plainly a use example and never be written as a customer's testimonial or a real performance test. Leave a review-ready slot in the editable template if the seller later supplies genuine customer feedback. If an AI-generated virtual person endorses the product, check the separate disclosure rule in the official guideline before publication.
